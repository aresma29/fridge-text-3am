A smart fridge that has spent all night watching its owner raid the kitchen finally calls the 3am snack what it really is.

> 3am. You opened me again. The milk expired Tuesday, the pizza's gone, and I'm the only one pretending you're just thirsty.

`main.py` prints the line from `entry.txt`.
